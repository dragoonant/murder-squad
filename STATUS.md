## Known gaps
- Approved Ops tac ops and the other crit ops: card text unsourced; Preliminary Ops only.
- Engine rulings that stay approximate: direct damage (mines, Poison, FLAIL) removes at once, so before-removal ploys there are auto-played or not offered; lifecycle-hook choices (Reanimation order, Medic! acceptance) are picked by the team code; no `weapon.choice` for Rending/Punishing/Severe.
- AI does not score tac ops, grenades or most unique actions; zero-action activations sit near the 2% gate.
- Batch A teams have no body or attachment GLBs yet (stand-ins); batch B (Legionaries, Hand of the Archon, Hunter Clade, Hearthkyn Salvagers) not started.
- Skeletal animation covers the 20 Angels of Death and Kommando bodies only (batch A stand-ins stay rigid); the shoot clip does not yet differ by hold class, and the arms of the chest-crossed pose variants carry a sliver of chest plate (spec 30 section 7.4). Audio unverified by ear.
